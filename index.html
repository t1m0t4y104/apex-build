FAQ

Common questions.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I'll design your homepage and show it to you. If it's not for you, no hard feelings and nothing owed.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your work, your list of services and the areas you cover. If you're missing something, I'll help you sort it out.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The website is a one-time cost. The Care Plan is optional. Without it, your site is hosted for free and you pay only for your domain.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Absolutely. A redesign works the same way: I build your new site first, then switch it over, keeping your domain and email exactly as they are.

Will I show up on Google?

Every site gets the technical basics Google looks for: fast loading, proper titles and descriptions, and pages for your services and areas. Ranking also depends on your Google Business Profile and reviews, and I'll show you how to strengthen both. Nobody can honestly guarantee a #1 spot.

What if I need changes later?

Care Plan clients just email me. Without the plan, small changes like updating text, photos, prices or hours are $49 each, and bigger additions like a new page are quoted upfront. Either way, you're never locked out of your own site.
