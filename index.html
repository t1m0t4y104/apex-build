FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. We'll design your homepage and show it to you. If it's not for you, no hard feelings and nothing owed.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your work, your list of services and the areas you cover. If you're missing something, we'll help you sort it out.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The website is a one-time cost. The Care Plan is optional. Without it, your site is hosted for free and you pay only for your domain.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Absolutely. Most of our work is rebuilding old or slow sites. We can keep your domain and email exactly as they are.

Will I show up on Google?

Every site gets the technical basics Google looks for: fast loading, proper titles and descriptions, and pages for your services and areas. Ranking also depends on your Google Business Profile and reviews, and we'll show you how to strengthen both. Nobody can honestly guarantee a #1 spot.

What if I need changes later?

Care Plan clients just email us. Without the plan, small changes are billed at a simple flat rate. Either way, you're never locked out of your own site.
