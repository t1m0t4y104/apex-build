Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

The short version: we only collect what we need to build and support your website, we never sell it, and we don't send marketing emails.

Apex Build is run by Tim Sommers in Okotoks, Alberta. If you have any questions about your information, email tim@apexbuildweb.ca.

What we collect

  • Mockup requests: your name, business name, email, and anything else you choose to fill in, like your phone number, trade, current website and notes.
  • Emails, calls and texts: whatever you send us when you get in touch.
  • Client projects: the content you send for your website, like your logo, photos, services and business details.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see or store your full card number. For e-transfers, we see your name and the amount, like any bank transfer.

How we use it

  • To make your free mockup and reply to your questions.
  • To build, launch and support your website.
  • To send invoices and keep the business records the law requires.

That's it. We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we won't add you to a mailing list.

Services we use

A few trusted companies help us run things, and they handle your information only to do that job:

  • Web3Forms delivers mockup requests from this site to our inbox.
  • Google runs our email, and Cloudflare routes it and hosts websites.
  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • Stripe processes card payments.

We also use a few other software tools to help write and build websites, and the content you send for your site may pass through them.

Some of these companies store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it is protected by their own security and privacy practices.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use tracking cookies, ads or analytics. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives basic technical details like your IP address so it can deliver them.

If you use the website speed test, the web address you type in is sent to Google PageSpeed Insights so Google can test it. Nothing else you enter is sent, and the result isn't saved.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it. Client records, like invoices and payments, are kept for six years because Canadian tax law requires it.

Keeping it safe

Our accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification, and only Apex Build and the services listed above handle your information.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we have about you, correct it, or have it deleted (except records we're required to keep). Just email tim@apexbuildweb.ca and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
